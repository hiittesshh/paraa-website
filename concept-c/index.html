What our advisors are allowed to tell you

This is the card every partner signs. It's public, so if you hear the right-hand column from anyone using our name, tell us.

The partner card: what an advisor may say, and what they must never say
Say Never say
  • "Your investment is held and valued in US dollars."
  • "You'll earn dollar appreciation."
  • "If the rupee strengthens, your rupee value falls."
  • "The rupee only goes down."
  • "Here's the full NAV history, including the bad months."
  • "It's returned X%" (with a period they picked).
  • "Here are the fees in rupees for your amount."
  • "There are no fees."
  • "I'm a distributor; I'm paid by Paraa. Here's how much."
  • "I'm your adviser" (unless SEBI-registered as one).
  • "I can't tell you how it's taxed; ask your CA. Here's the tax pack."
  • "It's tax-free" or "It avoids the 30% tax."
  • "Withdrawals take about [N] business days." TO CONFIRM
  • "You can take it out anytime, instantly."

AuditorAug 2026Illustrative How do you price a holding when its market is closed?

Institutional reviewerJun 2026Illustrative Can one person change the NAV?
No. The administrator computes it, and two of our staff must approve any revision.

We publish only what we're permitted to. Absence of an entry doesn't mean no questions were asked.
