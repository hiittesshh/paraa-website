The 2-minute risk brief

About 170 words · under 2 min

  1. You can lose money. Some of what you put in may not come back.
  2. Your investment is held and valued in US dollars. A stronger rupee lowers its rupee value, even if the investment didn't move.
  3. The investment itself can fall. Basket 1's weakest month was −6.3% and its deepest fall was −11.8% Illustrative
  4. Both can happen at once. In a year like that, ₹1,00,000 could become about ₹78,731 Illustrative
  5. Getting out takes [N] business days TO CONFIRM, and costs 1% if you've held under 12 months Illustrative
  6. Your money passes through [named companies] TO CONFIRM. If one fails, here's what happens: How your money moves.
  7. Rules on sending money abroad and on tax can change. We can't promise they won't.

That's it. If any of these is a deal-breaker, stop here, and that's a good outcome.

Each risk, in depth

In order of how often they bite (our judgement, Illustrative). Every ₹ example starts from ₹1,00,000 and is Illustrative.

1. Currency 4/5
What it is
Your investment is valued in US dollars. A weaker rupee raises its rupee value. A stronger rupee lowers it.
Worked ₹ example
The basket is flat in dollars and the rupee strengthens 5%: ₹1,00,000 becomes ₹95,000 before fees, a loss of ₹5,000 Illustrative
What we do
We show the currency effect on its own line on every statement.
What we can't do
We can't control it, and we don't try to predict it.
2. Market 4/5
What it is
The basket's holdings fall in price. Its NAV (the price of one unit of the basket) falls with them.
Worked ₹ example
Basket 1's weakest month was −6.3%: ₹1,00,000 would have become ₹93,698. Its deepest drawdown (the fall from a peak to the lowest point after it) was −11.8%, or ₹88,230, and took 14 months to recover Illustrative
What we do
Published limits on what a basket may hold TO CONFIRM, listed below.
What we can't do
We can't stop markets falling, or tell you when a fall will end.
3. Getting out 2/5
What it is
Withdrawals take [N] business days TO CONFIRM, longer around Indian and US holidays.
Worked ₹ example
You invest ₹1,00,000 and withdraw after 6 months: conversion in ₹500, management fee ₹744, exit fee ₹988, conversion out ₹489. You'd receive about ₹97,280 (assumed return 0%). Illustrative
What we do
An exit estimator with both countries' holiday calendars.
What we can't do
We can't make bank holidays or settlement days go faster.
4. A company in the chain fails 1/5
What it is
A bank, administrator or custodian has a problem.
Worked ₹ example
What happens to ₹1,00,000 depends on how your money is legally routed and held TO CONFIRM. We won't show a number until that is confirmed.
What we do
Every party named; what happens is on How your money moves TO CONFIRM
What we can't do
We can't promise every company in the chain stays sound.
5. Rules change 2/5
What it is
Indian rules on sending money abroad, or its tax, change.
Worked ₹ example
If TCS (tax collected at source, taken when money is sent abroad) applies at [rate] TO CONFIRM above [threshold] TO CONFIRM, part of your ₹1,00,000 is collected up front.
What we do
We follow the rule changes and tell you in the log.
What we can't do
We can't promise rules stay the same.
