Methodology

How we calculate what you see

What NAV means
NAV (net asset value) is the value of everything in the basket, minus what it owes, divided by the number of units. It's the price of one unit.
How returns are measured
Time-weighted (TWRR). It measures the basket, not the timing of anyone's deposits. Net of management fees. In US dollars unless labelled ₹.
Which exchange rate we use for ₹ figures
[Source] reference rate on the NAV date TO CONFIRM. Shown on every statement.
Who checks it
Administrator TO CONFIRM computes it. Auditor TO CONFIRM reviews it annually. Report download TO CONFIRM.
What we will never do
Remove a month from the history. Restart a track record. Show a period we chose because it looks good.
